Founded in 2022, Hotel Military Sultan quickly became the pride of Nandikotkur. With an unwavering passion for authentic Andhra cuisine, we have already served thousands of satisfied customers.

Our secret masala — each spice measure perfected with expertise — makes every meal here a royal experience. From our legendary Dum Biryani to crispy Chicken Roast, every dish carries the warmth of tradition.
