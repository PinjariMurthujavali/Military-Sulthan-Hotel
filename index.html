Our Story

A Legacy of
Royal Taste

Since 1985, Hotel Military Sultan has stood as the pride of Nandikotkur. Founded with a passion for authentic Andhra cuisine, we have served over a million satisfied customers over our 39-year journey.

Our secret family recipes — each spice measure perfected over decades — make every meal here a royal experience. From our legendary Dum Biryani to crispy Chicken Roast, every dish carries the warmth of tradition.
